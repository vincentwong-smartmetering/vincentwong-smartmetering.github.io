Intellectual Property Profile

Patent Number: MY-211193-A
Named Inventor: WONG YEW HON (Vincent Wong)
Patent Assignee: George Kent (Malaysia) Berhad

This patent defines a specialized hardware subsystem designed to convert mechanical counter rotations into highly precise electrical pulse data, establishing foundational telemetry components for regional smart infrastructure networks.

The Industry Challenge

Traditional reed-switch configurations suffer from mechanical jitter, parts free-play, and water hammer effects. They generate false forward increments during backflow, compromising the validity of meter data.

The Patented Configuration

An ultra-low power dual-sensor architecture and state-machine array capable of tracking phase lag/lead signatures. This allows the system to isolate rotation direction, ensure jitter immunity, and double resolution to 4 pulses per turn.
